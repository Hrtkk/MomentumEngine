# Financial metric glossary — BSE examples

Cutoff14 October2026. Historical figures are consolidated unless stated. cr = INR crore. Source IDs are in the appendix. CALCULATED figures inherit source limitations. Missing histories and peers are explicit, not zero.

## Operating revenue

**Definition:** Income from activities classified as the business operations.

**Formula / convention:** Sum transaction charges, listing fees, clearing-fund treasury income and other operating services.

**Current value / source:** Q1FY27 INR1,566.02cr, consolidated; S2 PDF5.

**History:** Q4FY26 1,563.51; Q1FY26 957.95; FY25 2,957.34; FY26 4,833.95. Earlier full series unresolved.

**Peers / benchmark:** MCX FY26 INR2,302cr, P1 PDF13; NSE consolidated figure missing.

**BSE interpretation:** Fast YoY growth but near-flat latest quarter; investigate product drivers.

**Limitations:** Operating classification includes some treasury income; not synonymous with pure fees.

**Assessment:** Improving YoY; stable sequentially

## Total income

**Definition:** Operating revenue plus investment and other income.

**Formula / convention:** Operating revenue + investment income + other income.

**Current value / source:** Q1FY27 INR1,706.72cr; S2 PDF5.

**History:** FY21–FY26: 654.7,863.5,953.9,1,596.0,3,236.3,5,148.1cr, rounded S3 PDF29.

**Peers / benchmark:** FY26 NSE18,713 rounded; MCX2,429.05cr; P2/P1.

**BSE interpretation:** Captures overall income scale; not all growth reflects customer demand.

**Limitations:** Investment returns and classifications can distort comparisons.

**Assessment:** Improving over observed years

## Growth and CAGR

**Definition:** Growth measures change; CAGR is the smooth annual rate linking endpoints.

**Formula / convention:** Period growth = current/prior −1; CAGR = (end/start)^(1/number of years) −1.

**Current value / source:** Q1FY27 revenue +63.5%YoY, +0.16%QoQ; CALCULATED S2.

**History:** FY21–FY26 total-income CAGR51.1%, five intervals; S3.

**Peers / benchmark:** FY26 operating growth BSE63.5%, MCX106.9%; NSE missing.

**BSE interpretation:** Small starting bases and new products can produce large growth.

**Limitations:** CAGR hides volatility; unlike annual revenue, quarters have seasonality.

**Assessment:** Improving annual scale; latest quarter stable

## Operating EBITDA

**Definition:** Operating earnings before interest, tax and depreciation, after regulatory and SGF expense here.

**Formula / convention:** Pre-associate PBT − investment/other income + depreciation + finance cost.

**Current value / source:** FY26 INR3,078.69cr; CALCULATED S1.

**History:** FY25 approximately1,499.72cr; longer harmonised series unavailable.

**Peers / benchmark:** MCX FY26 1,646.58cr after excluding other income; P1.

**BSE interpretation:** Shows core earning capacity before depreciation and tax.

**Limitations:** Not cash flow; excludes the cost of replacing assets through depreciation.

**Assessment:** Improving

## Operating EBITDA margin

**Definition:** Operating EBITDA retained per rupee of operating revenue.

**Formula / convention:** Operating EBITDA / operating revenue.

**Current value / source:** FY26 63.7%; Q1FY27 approximately66.8% from S2.

**History:** FY25 50.7%; older aligned series incomplete.

**Peers / benchmark:** MCX FY26 71.5%, FY25 59.8%; NSE missing.

**BSE interpretation:** For INR100 operating revenue, FY26 retains aboutINR63.7 before depreciation/tax.

**Limitations:** Do not compare directly with MCX73% total-income headline margin.

**Assessment:** Improving; below available MCX comparator

## Operating EBIT

**Definition:** Operating earnings after depreciation, before financing and tax.

**Formula / convention:** Operating EBITDA − depreciation.

**Current value / source:** FY26 INR2,919.73cr; CALCULATED S1.

**History:** FY25 approximately1,386.74cr. Longer aligned series unresolved.

**Peers / benchmark:** MCX FY26 INR1,568.54cr on aligned basis; P1.

**BSE interpretation:** Recognises consumption of technology and infrastructure assets.

**Limitations:** Useful lives and capitalisation policies affect depreciation.

**Assessment:** Improving

## Investment income

**Definition:** Returns booked on invested assets, separate from operating revenue in this model.

**Formula / convention:** Use financial statement line; separate realised gains, valuation changes and interest where available.

**Current value / source:** Q1FY27 INR135.18cr; S2.

**History:** Q4FY26 61.69; Q1FY26 79.06; FY26 290.30cr. Five-year composition missing.

**Peers / benchmark:** Peer detailed investment composition not reconciled.

**BSE interpretation:** Explains much of sequential income growth.

**Limitations:** Income may be non-cash or rate-sensitive; not all balances belong freely to owners.

**Assessment:** Higher; sustainability insufficient evidence

## Effective tax rate

**Definition:** Accounting tax expense relative to profit before tax.

**Formula / convention:** Total tax / reported PBT including associate contribution, for the historical ratio.

**Current value / source:** FY26 24.97%; CALCULATED S1.

**History:** FY25 24.66%; longer series unresolved.

**Peers / benchmark:** Comparable peer taxes before/after associate profits not reconciled.

**BSE interpretation:** Useful for checking forecast taxes and unusual tax effects.

**Limitations:** Associate profits already bear tax; deferred/prior-period tax distorts the ratio. Scenarios tax pre-associate profit.

**Assessment:** Broadly stable

## Parent PAT

**Definition:** Profit after tax allocated to BSE shareholders.

**Formula / convention:** Group PAT − profit attributable to non-controlling owners.

**Current value / source:** Q1FY27 INR874.02cr; group872.66 less minority loss−1.36; S2.

**History:** Q4FY26 797.33; Q1FY26 539.41; FY26 2,496.98; FY25 1,325.89cr.

**Peers / benchmark:** Peer tables use group PAT separately; matching parent series incomplete.

**BSE interpretation:** Correct earnings basis for shareholder EPS.

**Limitations:** Exceptional/discontinued gains and minority losses affect comparability.

**Assessment:** Improving

## Net profit margin

**Definition:** Share of income retained as after-tax group profit.

**Formula / convention:** Total-operation GROUP PAT / total income, consistently for comparison.

**Current value / source:** FY26 48.3%; CALCULATED S1.

**History:** FY25 40.9%; long adjusted chart is not a uniform reported series.

**Peers / benchmark:** NSE FY26 55.1%; MCX54.8% using arithmetic definition, P2/P1.

**BSE interpretation:** Shows conversion of all income to group profit, not purely operating efficiency.

**Limitations:** Mixes non-operating and exceptional effects; differs from issuer-defined margins.

**Assessment:** Improving; below both observed peers

## EPS and diluted share basis

**Definition:** Profit attributable to each share after the relevant share-count convention.

**Formula / convention:** Parent PAT / weighted-average eligible shares; basic and diluted published together here.

**Current value / source:** Q1FY27 INR21.22; S2.

**History:** FY26 60.61; FY25 32.18; Q4FY26 19.35. Historical adjusted chart not merged.

**Peers / benchmark:** MCX FY26 INR52.22 after its share-unit adjustment; raw EPS level is not a value ranking.

**BSE interpretation:** Per-share earnings matter more than total profit when dilution changes.

**Limitations:** Abeyance, bonuses, splits and adjusted versus total-operation definitions must match.

**Assessment:** Improving; capital reconciliation open

## Trailing EPS and P/E

**Definition:** Trailing EPS covers the latest four quarters; P/E is price per rupee of that earnings.

**Formula / convention:** TTM EPS = FY26 − Q1FY26 + Q1FY27; P/E = quote / TTM EPS.

**Current value / source:** INR68.74 and49.1x at provisionalINR3,375.40,13Oct2026; S1/S2/S5/S6.

**History:** FY26-basis55.7x; annualised latest-quarter39.8x is a sensitivity. Historical range unavailable.

**Peers / benchmark:** Matched-date NSE/MCX valuation unavailable; percentile not calculated.

**BSE interpretation:** A high multiple requires durable growth or other advantages to justify it.

**Limitations:** Official quote unverified; trailing profit is not a forward forecast.

**Assessment:** Insufficient evidence on attractiveness

## ROE

**Definition:** Profit earned relative to shareholders capital.

**Formula / convention:** Annual parent PAT / average opening and closing parent equity.

**Current value / source:** FY26 45.0%; 2,496.98 / average(4,423.73,6,673.01); S1.

**History:** Prior year average-equity series not reconciled; no five-year trend claimed.

**Peers / benchmark:** Aligned peer average-equity data unavailable.

**BSE interpretation:** High observed profitability relative to accounting capital.

**Limitations:** Distributions, small equity and exceptional gains can boost ROE. Ending-equity ROE differs.

**Assessment:** Insufficient trend evidence; strong current observation

## ROA

**Definition:** Profit relative to all recorded assets.

**Formula / convention:** Annual group PAT / average opening and closing group assets.

**Current value / source:** FY26 20.9%; CALCULATED S1.

**History:** Earlier aligned series not built.

**Peers / benchmark:** Peer clearing-asset conventions unresolved.

**BSE interpretation:** Provides a broad capital-intensity diagnostic.

**Limitations:** Clearing collateral and earmarked funds make industrial-bank comparisons misleading.

**Assessment:** Insufficient comparative evidence

## ROIC / ROCE

**Definition:** Return on capital employed in operations.

**Formula / convention:** ROIC = normalised after-tax operating profit / average operating invested capital; ROCE = EBIT / average defined capital employed.

**Current value / source:** — UNRESOLVED: operating capital cannot yet be isolated reliably.

**History:** — Five-year harmonised series unavailable.

**Peers / benchmark:** — No peer median or rank.

**BSE interpretation:** Would help test returns on technology and retained capital.

**Limitations:** Restricted cash, float, regulatory capital and associates need explicit treatment.

**Assessment:** Insufficient evidence

## Operating cash flow (CFO)

**Definition:** Cash generated by operating activity after working-capital changes and cash taxes.

**Formula / convention:** Reported continuing cash-flow statement under indirect method.

**Current value / source:** FY26 INR3,103.68cr; S1 printed154.

**History:** FY25 INR410.15cr; earlier series unresolved.

**Peers / benchmark:** Peer matched cash-flow basis unavailable.

**BSE interpretation:** Cash generation improved; inspect liability contribution.

**Limitations:** Settlement/earmarked balances and timing can obscure shareholder cash.

**Assessment:** Improving, with material qualifications

## Cash conversion

**Definition:** How much accounting profit is accompanied by operating cash.

**Formula / convention:** Continuing group CFO / continuing group PAT; multi-year uses sums, not average ratios.

**Current value / source:** FY26 1.25x; original model.

**History:** FY25 0.31x; combined two-year0.93x. No five-year series.

**Peers / benchmark:** Peer clearing/continuing basis incomplete.

**BSE interpretation:** A two-year view moderates the strong latest-year reading.

**Limitations:** Different cash-tax timing, associate income and liability flows affect the ratio.

**Assessment:** Improving annually; sustainability unresolved

## Capex and CFO less capex

**Definition:** Cash invested in infrastructure; residual after those purchases.

**Formula / convention:** CFO − cash purchases of PPE/intangibles/CWIP/capital advances, no assumed maintenance split.

**Current value / source:** FY26 capexINR515.16cr; CFO less capexINR2,588.52cr; S1.

**History:** FY25 153.23 and256.92cr respectively. Long history incomplete.

**Peers / benchmark:** Peer cash-capex definitions not reconciled.

**BSE interpretation:** Shows investment demands alongside cash generation.

**Limitations:** Not distributable cash; ignores extra regulatory/clearing capital and acquisitions.

**Assessment:** Investment rising; distributability unresolved

## Receivable days

**Definition:** Simplified number of revenue-days tied up in receivables.

**Formula / convention:** Ending net trade receivables / annual operating revenue ×365.

**Current value / source:** FY26 41.8days; S1.

**History:** FY25 35.5days; earlier series unavailable.

**Peers / benchmark:** Peer receivable/revenue mix not matched.

**BSE interpretation:** Collection absorption rose relative to revenue; investigate ageing.

**Limitations:** Ending balances, non-credit revenue and mix distort exact collection duration.

**Assessment:** Deteriorating diagnostic, not proof of credit loss

## Owned versus restricted cash

**Definition:** Balances held for company use versus designated obligations.

**Formula / convention:** Classify by legal entity, restriction and linked liability before estimating excess cash.

**Current value / source:** Owned-bank subtotalINR3,001.11cr excluding accrued interest; original model S1 notes11–12.

**History:** No consistent three-to-five-year usable-cash series.

**Peers / benchmark:** Peer required-capital treatment unresolved.

**BSE interpretation:** A large cash balance does not imply an equal special dividend.

**Limitations:** Owned is not necessarily excess; reserve and operating needs remain.

**Assessment:** Insufficient evidence on excess cash

## Contingencies

**Definition:** Disclosed possible obligations, not necessarily probable losses.

**Formula / convention:** Sum separately disclosed claim and guarantee amounts; do not add provisions again.

**Current value / source:** FY26 aggregateINR330.16cr; S1 note37, original model.

**History:** FY25 corresponding aggregateINR239.08cr; note37.

**Peers / benchmark:** Peer legal exposures not standardised.

**BSE interpretation:** Larger disclosed exposure warrants scrutiny of cases and collateral.

**Limitations:** No loss probability or timing is established by the aggregate.

**Assessment:** Exposure increased; outcome unresolved

## Premium ADTV and fee yield

**Definition:** Average daily option premium traded; fee earned per unit of chargeable activity.

**Formula / convention:** ADTV = premium turnover / trading days; yield = relevant revenue / correctly scoped chargeable turnover.

**Current value / source:** Q1FY27 premium ADTVINR29,615.4cr/day, S3 PDF13. Net yield not computed.

**History:** Q4FY26 INR28,920.7cr/day; longer like-for-like share/yield series pending.

**Peers / benchmark:** NSE Q4FY26 INR76,375cr/day per P2; do not mix Q4 with BSE Q1.

**BSE interpretation:** More relevant than notional turnover for options fee analysis.

**Limitations:** Trading days, one/two-sided charging, mix and discounts need reconciliation.

**Assessment:** Activity improving; fee yield unresolved

## Dividends and scenario returns

**Definition:** Cash distributed to owners and conditional wealth changes.

**Formula / convention:** Dividend yield = comparable DPS/price; model wealth = terminal EPS×P/E + cumulative unreinvested DPS.

**Current value / source:** Historical yield not asserted; scenario payout capped at25%profit and cash proxy.

**History:** Actual cash dividendsFY25INR205.99cr; FY26INR315.85cr, S1.

**Peers / benchmark:** Peer dividend policies and record dates not aligned.

**BSE interpretation:** Dividends supplement price return but depend on available cash.

**Limitations:** Scenario wealth CAGR is not cash-flow IRR; taxes/reinvestment excluded.

**Assessment:** Scenario assumption; future payout unresolved
