# BSE Ltd — interpretation and intelligence report

**Research cutoff: 14 October 2026, Asia/Kolkata.** Financial baseline: FY26 and Q1 FY27 through June2026. Market reference: secondary quote timestamped 13 October2026, 15:59 IST. This report expands the original evidence pack without replacing its assumptions or unresolved questions. It is not a claim of complete current-event coverage.

## 1. Business economics: what shareholders own

BSE earns fees from the infrastructure around securities trading, listings, clearing, distribution and information services. Its economics differ from a bank: customer trading activity and the price charged for infrastructure matter more than loan growth or loan-loss ratios. Debt/equity and an industrial cash-conversion cycle are not sufficient ways to assess it.

The working investment hypothesis is that increased liquidity attracts additional participants, who improve the platform's usefulness and support recurring fee income. This feedback can create a competitive advantage. The counterargument is that liquidity can concentrate on another venue, regulation can change trading behaviour, and competition can reduce fees. **UNRESOLVED hypothesis:** the historical growth does not by itself prove the strength or permanence of this advantage.

Our operational model therefore separates industry premium activity, BSE's relative share and effective fee yield. Other operating businesses grow separately. It does not assume a rising stock index automatically produces proportionate exchange revenue. What matters is chargeable activity, mix and monetisation, including activity outside a single expiry window. Monitor product-level participation, turnover, price schedules and concentration.

NSE is the relevant direct competitor; MCX is an exchange-economics comparator. NSE's broader scale makes it useful for testing the competitive hypothesis, while MCX demonstrates how a different derivatives mix can change growth and costs. Peer figures and limitations are in section04.

This bridge prevents three mistakes: calling total income operating sales, calling group profit parent earnings, and treating associate earnings as a dividend received.

Sequential profit improved faster than operating revenue. Investment income rose by INR73.49 crore; depreciation fell and other expenses fell, while employee and technology costs increased. The August call attributes some previous-quarter other expense to a debtor provision (MANAGEMENT CLAIM, S4 printed7). A provision is an accounting charge for an expected loss. Its absence in the next quarter is not the same thing as recovery of the debt.

For BSE, the next test is whether transaction income and operating profit advance without reliance on investment gains or lower exceptional charges. Peer margins must be reconciled before making this comparison: MCX includes other income in its headline EBITDA, whereas BSE also presents an operating measure. The harmonised calculation shows BSE's FY26 operating margin below MCX's, despite BSE's own improvement.

## 3. Balance sheet: cash, obligations and shareholder capital

The balance sheet records what the group owns and owes at a date. It does not tell us that all bank balances can be paid to shareholders. Exchange clearing operations involve earmarked funds, obligations to members and regulatory requirements.

S1 notes11–12 identify owned and earmarked balances. The original model's INR3,001.11 crore owned-bank-balance subtotal excludes accrued interest and does not establish excess cash. Legal-entity allocation, minimum required resources and operating liquidity must still be reconciled. We consequently make no excess-cash addition to the P/E scenarios and no enterprise-value claim.

Receivables rose faster than operating revenue. Ending receivables/revenue ×365 increased from approximately 35.5 days in FY25 to 41.8 days in FY26 (CALCULATED, S1 balance sheet). This is a simplified collection diagnostic, not exact credit-sales days: year-end timing and revenue mix can distort it. It warrants reconciliation to ageing and impairment, not an accusation of poor accounting.

FY26 parent return on average equity is approximately 45.0%; return on average total assets is approximately 20.9%. Definitions and matching profit bases are explicit in the glossary. Exchange collateral and restricted assets complicate cross-company asset comparisons. High returns on equity may reflect strong economics, a small equity denominator, distributions or unusual profit; they are not automatically a reason to pay any price.

Contingencies are potential obligations whose outcome or timing is uncertain. The original model totals disclosed claims and guarantees, but does not treat that amount as an expected loss or subtract it mechanically from value. The risk register specifies what additional evidence would make a contingency economically material.

The share basis also needs care. Reported EPS includes abeyance entitlements; paid-up shares and the presentation's outstanding-share count differ. A bonus issue increases share count while proportionately reducing per-share units; it does not itself create economic value. Future actual dilution, by contrast, can reduce each existing share's claim on earnings. Scenarios therefore state their dilution assumptions explicitly.

## 4. Cash flow: does accounting profit become useful cash?

Operating cash flow adjusts accounting profit for non-cash items, taxes and movements in amounts owed or owing. BSE's group cash flow cannot be equated with cash freely distributable by the parent.

The FY26 continuing cash-flow bridge is reproducible: cash before working-capital movements INR3,172.93 crore, minus receivable absorption INR305.95 crore, plus payable/provision/other-asset effects INR164.44 crore, plus other-liability movement INR727.67 crore, less cash tax INR655.41 crore, gives INR3,103.68 crore. **CALCULATED from S1 printed154.**

A liability increase can temporarily leave more cash in the business. Before forecasting that cash, ask whether it is recurring operating funding, settlement-related cash, a temporary payable, or a balance that reverses. Removing that single contribution produces a diagnostic, not a corrected accounting result; other movements may be economically connected.

CFO minus cash capex is a useful first approximation, but can overstate distributable cash if the exchange must replenish required funds, retain clearing liquidity, or finance additional investment. Conversely, growth capex may support future earning capacity. The model separates capex from depreciation and additional capital lock-up from income-statement SGF expense, preventing double subtraction of the same expense.

Peer cash conversion is incomplete because we have not reconciled identical clearing-fund and continuing-operation definitions across NSE and MCX. There is no verified best-in-class cash-conversion claim here.

## 5. History and business-cycle interpretation

The six-year total-income series shows substantial expansion, with uneven yearly growth. Secular participation in financial markets is a plausible supporting mechanism, but the share of growth attributable to structural participation, BSE share gains, fee changes and favourable trading conditions remains unquantified.

A cyclical increase in trading activity can produce unusually strong margins through operating leverage; a reversal can operate in the opposite direction. Disposal gains and discontinued operations additionally distort profit history. The historical panel deliberately identifies these effects instead of combining adjusted and reported EPS into a smooth series.

Capital allocation requires judging both returns on infrastructure spending and distributions to owners. Increased technology spending may protect capacity and resilience, or may merely be necessary to remain competitive. The cash-flow trend and the management discussion support investigation, not a completed return-on-investment assessment. Five-year ownership changes, adjusted share-price returns and valuation ranges remain explicitly missing.

## 6. Valuation and future operating scenarios

The reported trailing P/E is approximately49.1x using a provisional quote; this is not a verified live multiple. The denominator is reported total-operation EPS, with consistent periods. Annualising Q1 produces a lower multiple, but assumes that quarter's earnings repeat.

Section05 builds bear, base and bull operating paths. The starting annual revenue capacity is four times the June quarter, not a forecast FY27 result. Derivatives revenue is driven by industry activity, relative share and net fee yield; fixed and variable costs are modelled separately. Taxes, associate earnings, dilution, capex, incremental working capital and additional capital reserves are explicit.

Each scenario produces annual earnings, EPS, conditional prices and cash-dividend wealth at one, three and five years. Terminal multiples are assumptions, not peer-derived fair values. The model's wide range highlights sensitivity and cannot be compressed into one reliable expected price without defensible probabilities. No probability weighting is supplied.

The current base path has positive operating growth, stable fee yield and no dilution. It could be too optimistic if regulation reduces participation or if more spending is required; it could be too conservative if new services deliver meaningful recurring profit. Management's plans and our assumptions are kept separate. No independently reconciled analyst consensus has been obtained.

## 7. Business quality, management and risks

Network participation, infrastructure reliability and efficient capital use are the potential strengths to test. The major risks are volume concentration, fee competition, regulation, operational outages, clearing/member exposures, capital restrictions and valuation compression. These risks can interact: a rule change may affect both volumes and the multiple that investors pay.

Management's explanations of provisions, future data distribution and regulatory uncertainty are useful research inputs. They are not substitutes for a guidance-versus-delivery history. Governance quality cannot be certified from an unmodified financial audit alone. A full auditor-change history, related-party review and regulatory-event chronology are not complete.

The risk register separates observed facts from hypothetical adverse scenarios, and ties each to warning indicators and thesis invalidation conditions. It supplies research actions rather than trading triggers. No automation or portfolio change is created by this report.
