# BSE research monitoring — proposed manual checklist

Created 14 October 2026. Research horizon assumption: 3–5 years. These are research review conditions, not trading instructions. No automated monitoring has been enabled.

| Check | Baseline / source | Review condition | When | Missing-data behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Premium turnover and fee yield | S3 PDF13; current baseline ends June2026 | Two consecutive monthly declines in comparable ADTV, or fee schedule change; investigate seasonality and market-wide moves before drawing conclusions | Next: obtain July–September2026 data; thereafter monthly | Mark unknown; never infer zero or stable activity |
| Product/expiry concentration | Not yet established | Material concentration or migration; assess using monthly and non-expiry participation | With operational data | Keep durability hypothesis unconfirmed |
| Operating profitability | S2 PDF5; S3 PDF26 | Sequential operating EBITDA decline or at least 3 percentage-point margin contraction; check SGF and exceptional costs | Next reported quarter, date not verified | Await filing; do not annualise stale results |
| Earnings composition | Model inputs | Profit grows while operating revenue is flat/down; reconcile investment gains and provision changes | Each results release | Label unresolved |
| Cash conversion | Model output, continuing group basis | CFO below continuing group profit over two annual periods; any material liability reversal | Half-year and annual cash flows | Do not estimate quarter CFO from PAT |
| Restrictions and capital | S1 notes11–12,41–42 | New required capital or settlement-fund contribution; changed availability of cash | Each relevant filing | No excess-cash uplift in valuation |
| Regulatory changes | Current rule inventory incomplete | Any binding change affecting fees, margins, contracts, access, tax or expiries | On discovery, check issue AND effective date | A media report creates a verification task, not a rule assumption |
| Governance and operations | Initial check only | Auditor qualification/resignation, significant outage, regulatory order, debtor impairment or material related-party change | On disclosure | Preserve allegation/fact distinction |
| Per-share basis | S1 abeyance note; S2 EPS footnote | Share allotment, bonus, split or dilution | On disclosure | Block share-count-derived market cap until reconciled |
| Valuation assumptions | Indicative price only | Recompute after verified price/earnings updates; compare achievable EPS growth with sensitivity table | Quarterly/material event | Keep provisional status if price is not official |

Thresholds above are analyst-selected review aids, not statistically validated signals.

## Research queue, in priority order

1. Obtain July–September premium turnover, comparable NSE series, trading days and BSE fee schedules. Reconcile premium activity to realised transaction revenue. Owner: next researcher; before a durability conclusion.
2. Match archived company PDFs to issuer/NSE-hosted documents; obtain official 13 October close and latest capital reconciliation. Owner: next researcher; before a verified valuation snapshot.
3. Trace earmarked assets, clearing liabilities, regulatory capital and distributions by legal entity. Reconcile FY26 other-liability cash-flow contribution. Owner: next researcher; before DCF/EV.
4. Retrieve applicable regulator/tax documents behind the August call commentary; distinguish management interpretation from binding text. Owner: next researcher; before the next quarterly comparison.
5. Extend annual and quarterly history; normalise exceptional/discontinued profits and capital actions. Owner: next researcher; before treating this as a reference-standard full dossier.
6. Compare a small set of accessible broker assumptions and peer economics. Record estimate publication date, period, EPS basis and actual outcome. Owner: next researcher; after historical data is reconciled.

Append future events and thesis revisions with timestamps. Never replace an old forecast with a revised one when measuring forecast accuracy. Quiet periods require a completed source check; a failed feed is incomplete coverage.
