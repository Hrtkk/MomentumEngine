1) Note No. 6 of the Standalone Financial Results which states that the National Company Law
   Tribunal (NCLT) has initiated the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) against
   the key subsidiary namely Shrivallabh Pittie Enterprises Private Limited by passing an order
   dated 03.09.2026 and against the key step down subsidiaries namely Shri Vallabh Pittie
   South West Industries Limited and Shri Vallabh Pittie Industries Limited by passing an order
   dated 10.10.2023 and 07.03.2024 respectively, which are subsidiaries of Shrivallabh Pittie
   Enterprises Private Limited. As a part of CIRP of the Company, creditors had cast their vote
   for approving a Resolution Plan of Shri Vallabh Pittie South West Industries Limited and
   Shri Vallabh Pittie Industries Limited. That certain information including the minutes of
   meeting of the Committee of Creditors (“CoC”) and the outcome of certain procedures
   carried out as a part of the CIRP are confidential in nature and could not be shared with
   anyone other than the Committee of Creditors and NCLT. Accordingly, we are unable to
   comment on the possible financial impact, presentation, and disclosures, if any, that may
   arise if we had been provided access to that information.


2) Note No. 7 of Standalone Financial Results which states that Shrivallabh Enterprises Private
   Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of SVP Textiles Limited, was admitted into the
   Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) pursuant to an order passed by the National
   Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) dated 03.09.2026. Consequently, the management of
   Shrivallabh Enterprises Private Limited has been suspended and its affairs are being
   managed by the Resolution Professional (RP). Accordingly, SVP Global Textiles Limited
   does not exercise control over Shrivallabh Enterprises Private Limited. Further, all the
   downstream subsidiaries of SVP Global Textiles Limited, namely, Shrivallabh Pittie South
   West Industries Limited, Shrivallabh Pittie Industries Limited, SV Pittie Trading (FZC)
   LLC- Oman and SV Pittie Sohar Textiles (FZC) AOC- Oman are also under the control of
   the RP of Shrivallabh Enterprises Private Limited pursuant to the ongoing CIRP
   proceedings.

   In view of the above circumstances, the Company has not been provided with the necessary
   financial information, records and details of Shrivallabh Enterprises Private Limited and its
   downstream subsidiaries by the RP, which are required for the preparation of consolidated
   financial statements. Consequently, in the absence of such information and the Company’s
   inability to exercise control over the aforesaid entities, the Company is unable to prepare and
   present consolidated financial results for the relevant period.
