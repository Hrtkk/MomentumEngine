UltraTech Cement Limited, an Aditya Birla Group company, today announced its financial results for
the quarter ended 30 September 2026.

FINANCIALS
The results demonstrate the stability of UltraTech’s earnings, with revenue, operating profit and net
profit all growing by double digits. Consolidated Net Sales for the quarter rose 18.9% year-on-year to
Rs. 23,024 crore, from Rs. 19,371 crore in Q2 FY26. Profit before interest, depreciation and tax (PBIDT)
increased 17.3% to Rs. 3,832 crore, from Rs. 3,268 crore. Profit after tax (PAT) grew 21.3% to Rs. 1,501
crore, from Rs. 1,238 crore.

OPERATIONS
UltraTech performed well despite headwinds on account of a monsoon-affected quarter and the
ongoing west Asia crisis. Domestic grey cement sales volumes rose robust 13.8% to 35.94 million
tonnes, from 31.58 million tonnes in Q2 FY26, supported by disciplined execution, a wider market
footprint, cost optimisation and a sharper focus on premium products. Capacity utilisation jumped to
71% from 66% over the same period last year. Higher volumes and improved profitability strengthened
UltraTech’s position in the market.
During the quarter, UltraTech launched Ultravolt, its wires and cables business, adding a new category
to its Building Products portfolio. The business is building a strong distribution network as part of its Pan
India strategy. A state-of-the-art manufacturing facility has been commissioned at Jhagadia, Gujarat.
The overall planned capacity, entailing the consumption of 78000 mt of copper and 24000 mt of
Aluminium, already positions the Company as the second largest player in terms of capacity in the
housing wires segment. The launch has received an overwhelming market response. The brand has
garnered strong endorsement from retailers, electricians and industry influencers for its quality, safety
and performance-focused product portfolio.

MANUFACTURING CAPACITY
UltraTech continued to strengthen its manufacturing presence in the cement industry through capacity
additions, debottlenecking and operational excellence programmes. During the quarter, the Company
commissioned 3.6 mtpa greenfield integrated capacity at Petnikota, Andhra Pradesh, and added 1.0
mtpa through debottlenecking at its existing cement plants at Visakhapatnam, Patratu and Nathdwara.

As of 30 September 2026, the Company’s consolidated domestic grey cement capacity stood at 204.7
mtpa. Including 5.4 mtpa of overseas capacity, its global grey cement capacity stood at 210.1 mtpa.
The Company is on track with its organic growth capex programme to reach a capacity of 240+ mtpa by
March 2028.

SUSTAINABILITY
Sustainability remains central to UltraTech’s growth strategy. During the quarter, the Company added
117 mw renewable energy and 10 mw Waste Heat Recovery System (WHRS) capacity, raising the share
of green power in its energy mix.

At the end of this quarter, installed WHRS capacity stood at 444 MW and renewable power capacity at
1,580 MW. Green power, comprising WHRS and renewable energy, accounted for 48% of the
Company’s power mix for the quarter, giving the Company a unique position with 2 GW of green energy
capacity for captive use.

During the quarter, the Company joined the Leadership Group for Industry Transition (LeadIT), an
initiative launched by India and Sweden to accelerate decarbonisation of heavy industries.
