                    Q2 FY26-27 Consolidated Revenue Rs. 4,762 crore, up 26 % Y-o-Y

Mumbai, India – 14th October, 2026:
CEAT Limited (CIN No: L25100MH1958PLC011041), an RPG Group company, today announced its
unaudited results for the first quarter ended on 30th September, 2026.

On a consolidated basis, the Company’s revenue closed at Rs. 4,762 crore, an increase of
26% Y-o-Y, EBITDA margin stood at 9.5%. Net profit stood at Rs. 112 crore.

Commenting on the results as well as the outlook of the business, Mr. Arnab Banerjee, MD & CEO, CEAT
Limited, said, “In Q2, we delivered a strong double-digit revenue growth of 26% Y-o-Y, continuing the momentum
of the past several quarters, led by growth in premium segments across BUs. The West Asia crisis kept raw
material costs high and did present challenges, but we responded with tighter cost controls and disciplined
execution.
In the second half of the year, we shall look at delivering double digit growth again, alongside further price
hikes."

On a standalone basis, the Company’s revenue stood at Rs. 4548 crore, 23% Y-o-Y and EBITDA margin
stood at 10.14% and net profit was reported at Rs. 164 crore.

Mr. Kumar Subbiah, CFO of CEAT Limited, said, “The continuing conflict in West Asia kept commodity
costs elevated during the quarter. We will remain focused on calibrated pricing actions and improving
our product mix, with moderate price increases.
Debt increased during the quarter, largely due to higher capital expenditure of close to ₹490 crore to
increase our capacities where utilisation has been high and dividend payouts. In Q3, we expect cost
pressures to persist into Q3 as the commodity prices continue to remain high. However, we will continue
to maintain fiscal discipline while investing to meet demand and drive profitable growth.”

About CEAT Ltd (www.ceat.com):
CEAT, the flagship company of RPG Enterprises, was established in 1958. Today, CEAT is one of India’s
leading tyre manufacturers and has a strong presence in global markets. CEAT produces more than 41
million high-performance tyres, catering to various segments like 2-3 Wheelers, Passenger and Utility
Vehicles, Commercial Vehicles and Off-Highway Vehicles.

About RPG Group (www.rpggroup.com):
RPG Group, established in 1979, is one of India’s fastest-growing business groups with a turnover of US$
5.2 billion. The group has diverse business interests in the areas of Infrastructure, Tyres, Pharma, IT and
Specialty as well as in emerging innovation-led technology businesses.
