## Rule, in words

1. **Discovery.** Each session, the top-100 gainers of the Nifty 500 by day return. A name stays a candidate for 7 sessions ("persistence").
2. **Score (MQS, 0–100).** Percentiles among candidates of: relative strength (½·R21 + ½·R3) 25 · trend stack (close vs SMA20/50/200, SMA20 > SMA50 > SMA200) 15 · volume + delivery ratio 15 · breakout proximity (55- and 252-session highs) 15 · persistence 10 · sector (fixed at 50) 10 · low ATR/close 10. Deductions: −15 for a > 10% first-day spike, −10 for close > 1.15 × SMA20, −15 for a name below its SMA200 and below 65% of its 252-session high.
3. **Add** when MQS ≥ 70; **remove** when MQS < 50 or the name leaves the candidate set; **replace** the weakest holding (held ≥ 3 sessions) when a candidate beats it by ≥ 10 points, at most 2 swaps a day.
4. **Size.** Up to 13 names, target weight `1/max(7, N)`, whole shares, 20% per-name cap, one share allowed if `price ≤ 20% of equity`. No sector cap.
5. **Stop.** `close − 2 × ATR20`, must be within 8% of the close, fixed at entry; 5-session cooldown after a stop.
6. **Execution.** Decide at close, trade at the next open, 0.6% round-trip cost.

## Why a stock is picked

It was among the day's biggest gainers within the last 7 sessions **and** scored ≥ 70 on MQS. The `data/codex/picks_<date>.csv` file lists the score components per pick; the morning report (`reports/<date>.html`) prints them with the Codex bear case.
