## Rule, in words

1. **Rank (weekly).** On the last session of each week, rank every eligible Liquid-500 name by `R6 = C/C[t−126] − 1`. Rank 1 = highest.
2. **Enter.** Any name with rank ≤ 50 that is not held is a buy at the next open, in rank order, while slots (10) and the sector cap allow. Orders that find no slot lapse; the rule retries at the next refresh.
3. **Hold.** A holding stays while its rank is ≤ 125 at each weekly refresh (hysteresis: 50 in, 125 out). Nothing changes between refreshes except stops and data exits.
4. **Stop.** `close − 3 × ATR20`, fixed at entry, intraday.
5. **Exit (weekly).** Rank > 125 at a refresh → sell at the next open.
6. **Exit (monthly / data).** Left Liquid-500 at a month-end → sell. 5 sessions without a trade → sell.

No profit target, no indicator exits.

## Why a stock is picked

One fact only: **its 6-month price return is in the top 50 of about 500 liquid names.** `R6` and `rank` in the signal record are the whole reason. RSI, SMA20 distance, day return, volume and sector are recorded, not used.

## Sizing and costs

As M11 (common E2 rules). Stop distances of 11–17% are a property of these high-volatility names: 3 × ATR20 is wide on purpose (a *catastrophic* stop), it is not a trading stop.

Holdout 90% bootstrap interval of annual excess: **[−2.1%, +27.8%]** — it includes zero. Excess at 0.3 / 0.6 / 1.0% costs: +19.9 / +15.1 / +14.9%.

Plain reading: the highest raw return of the ladder, bought with the deepest drawdowns. It is the textbook momentum factor (Jegadeesh & Titman 1993) on an Indian point-in-time universe; its known failure mode is a momentum crash after a sharp market reversal.

## What it does not do

- No timing of the entry. It buys the top-50 the week they appear, whatever the chart looks like. The extension-veto variant (M9, on the 3-month rule) did not improve results — see M11.md "What it does not do".
- No market filter; no volatility scaling (M5/M6 tested those: M5 selected but not reliable, M6 failed).
- Dual-momentum variant M8 (only names beating the index with R6 > 0) was selected on development and was also "not reliable" on holdout for the same drawdown reason.
