### Why these changes matter

The bonus issue does not itself create more business value; it increases the share count while proportionately adjusting per-share economics. Our earlier 32.6x calculation used FY26 earnings. A secondary data provider subsequently reported approximately 24.5x P/E on a trailing earnings basis at its 9 October price snapshot. These are different earnings bases and price dates, so the apparent difference is not necessarily a contradiction.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://www.valueresearchonline.com\&sz=32)

Compare with Others



The restructuring requires a separate analysis. If these subsidiaries were already consolidated, merging them into the parent generally does not automatically introduce all their revenue as new group revenue. The investment case should focus on changes to ownership interests, financing, taxes, operations and future profitability—not double-counting existing subsidiary sales.

### What this reveals

Goodluck has demonstrated real multi-year earnings growth. Profit has grown far faster than revenue, consistent with improving profitability from a relatively low starting margin.

But there is a complication: ROCE has declined from approximately 17% in FY24 to 14% in FY26, even as reported operating profit has increased.

ROCE measures the operating return generated by the capital invested in the business. This may indicate that new factories and working capital are entering the balance sheet ahead of their full earnings contribution.

That's plausible for a business undergoing major expansion, but it must be tested. Higher future profits alone will not establish that the expansion creates attractive shareholder value.

If defence revenue reaches ₹300 crore, that would be more than six times FY26 revenue. But it would not mean consolidated company revenue grows sixfold.

The real assessment requires four measurements: confirmed orders and delivery schedules; realized profitability; capital required to generate that profitability; and the extent to which existing investors retain the resulting earnings after subsidiary fundraising and restructuring.

The 6 August disclosures also describe a proposed ₹285 crore preferential fundraising at the defence subsidiary. This creates an additional ownership-and-dilution question, especially alongside the October amalgamation proposal. We must establish whether the fundraising was completed, what ownership changed, and whether any minority shareholders could receive listed-parent shares under the proposed scheme.&#x20;

### Updated investment interpretation

Goodluck's strongest evidence is its five-year improvement in earnings and the recent recovery in quarterly growth.

Its biggest unresolved question is whether the investment program that is expanding its factories and higher-value product capacity will produce sustainable incremental ROCE and free cash flow.

A secondary risk now deserves equal attention: the precise terms of the proposed restructuring, including any effects on ownership, dilution and the economic interest of listed shareholders.

The near-term monitoring milestones are the September-quarter consolidated results, updated debt and working-capital disclosures, defence order execution, and the detailed amalgamation scheme.

I would not conclude that Goodluck is fairly valued, undervalued or overvalued until those issues and the direct-peer multiples are reconciled.

A recurring Goodluck research check could track these disclosures and alert you only when a meaningful thesis or risk indicator changes.
