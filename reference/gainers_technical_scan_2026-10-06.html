How to read this

A big day alone isn't a signal — about a quarter of today's gainers are bouncing inside a downtrend (below 200-DMA, far from highs), which often fades. The score (higher = better technical structure for a 1–4 week swing view) rewards: price above 20/50/200-DMA, 50-DMA above 200-DMA, within 5% of 52-week high or new high, breakout above pivot R2, MACD bullish crossover, RSI 55–72, closing in the top of the day's range and above VWAP, positive 1M & 3M returns. It penalises: below 200-DMA, >35% under 52W high, RSI >78, >15% above 20-DMA, >10% single-day jump, fading from the day's high. Ref. support = higher of 20-DMA and pivot S1 — a common place traders anchor a stop; Risk % = distance from price to that level (smaller = tighter stop possible). Breadth is weak (24% above 50-DMA), so even good setups face a headwind.
